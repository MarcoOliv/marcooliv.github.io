# Privacy Policy — MarkOne

**Last updated:** October 14, 2026

**MarkOne** is a habit tracking application developed by **Capucho Labs** ("we", "our", "us"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the MarkOne application ("App"), available on iOS, Android, macOS, and Web.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information you provide

- **Account data:** email address and password when creating an account.
- **Habit data:** name, icon, color, category, frequency, time, duration, and completion dates of the habits you create.
- **Checklists:** name, icon, color, and items of the lists you create.
- **Suggestions:** title, description, and author name (optional) when submitting suggestions.

### 1.2 Information collected automatically

- **Usage data (Firebase Analytics and usage summary):** we record how the App is used — days it was opened and how many times actions such as creating a list, adding or checking an item, registering a purchase, creating or checking a habit and viewing Premium happened — plus platform, app version and whether the account is Premium. This data is linked to your account's internal identifier (not your name or email) and is used to understand where the App can improve and to offer benefits such as complimentary Premium. We **never** record the content you type (names of lists, items, habits or events), amounts or prices. We do not use advertising identifiers (IDFA/AAID), do not track you across other apps or websites and do not share this data with advertisers.
- **Subscription data:** purchase information processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store, managed by RevenueCat. We do not have access to your complete credit card or payment method data.

### 1.3 Information we do NOT collect

- Geographic location
- Device contacts
- Photos, camera, or microphone
- Data from other apps
- Health information

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Provide and maintain the App's functionality
- Sync your data across devices (Premium feature)
- Process and manage your subscription
- Send local notifications (habit reminders and achievements)
- Improve the App based on aggregated usage data
- Manage the community suggestions system

---

## 3. Data Storage and Security

### 3.1 Local storage

Your habit data, checklists, and settings are stored locally on your device using SQLite. The App works 100% offline.

### 3.2 Cloud storage

If you create an account and have a Premium subscription, your data is synced with **Google Cloud Firestore** (a Firebase/Google Cloud service). Servers are located in the United States and follow Google Cloud Platform security standards.

### 3.3 Authentication

Account management is handled by **Firebase Authentication**. Your password is encrypted and we do not have access to it in plain text.

### 3.4 Payments

Subscriptions are processed directly by the **Apple App Store** or **Google Play Store** and managed by **RevenueCat**. We do not store payment data on our servers.

---

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

- **Firebase (Google):** for authentication, cloud storage, and analytics.
- **RevenueCat:** for subscription management.
- **Apple / Google:** for payment processing in their respective stores.

All mentioned third parties operate under their own privacy policies and security standards.

---

## 5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** your data at any time through the App.
- **Correct** your data by editing your habits and checklists.
- **Delete** your data by removing habits, checklists, or your account.
- **Export** your data (future feature).
- **Cancel** your subscription at any time through your store settings (App Store or Google Play).

To request complete deletion of your account and all associated data, contact us at: **capucholabs@gmail.com**

---

## 6. Data Retention

- **Local data:** remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.
- **Cloud data:** remains stored as long as your account exists. Upon requesting account deletion, all data will be removed within 30 days.
- **Usage data:** Firebase Analytics data follows the retention configured in Firebase; the account usage summary is removed together with your other data when you request account deletion.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

MarkOne is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not intentionally collect data from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete it.
