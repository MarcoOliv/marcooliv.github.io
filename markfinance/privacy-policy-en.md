# Privacy Policy — MarkFinance

**Last updated:** October 14, 2026

**MarkFinance** is a personal finance management application developed by **Capucho Labs** ("we", "our", "us"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the MarkFinance application ("App"), available on iOS, Android, and macOS.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information you provide

- **Account data:** email address and password when creating an account.
- **Financial data:** bank accounts (name, type, initial balance), credit cards (name, brand, limit, closing/due dates), transactions (income, expenses, transfers), categories, recurrences, and budgets that you record.
- **Suggestions:** title, description, and author name (optional) when submitting feature suggestions.

### 1.2 Information collected automatically

- **Usage metrics (Firebase Analytics):** we record usage events that are anonymous as to content — for example, "created an account", "added a transaction", "imported a statement", "opened the app today" — plus device information (model, operating system, app version). Events **never** include account or category names, descriptions, amounts or any text you type. They are linked only to an internal code for your account (no name or email), so we can count active users, understand where the app is hard to use and offer benefits such as complimentary Premium. A summary of these counts (days the app was opened, how many times each event happened, platform, version and subscription status) is kept in our database. **We do not use an advertising identifier, do not track you across other apps or websites and do not sell this data.**
- **Subscription data:** purchase information processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store, managed by RevenueCat. We do not have access to your complete credit card or payment method data.

### 1.3 Information we do NOT collect

- Geographic location
- Device contacts
- Photos, camera, or microphone
- Data from other apps
- Real banking credentials (login, password, or bank tokens)
- Health information

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Provide and maintain the App's functionality
- Sync your financial data across devices (Premium feature)
- Process and manage your subscription
- Provide financial assistance via AI assistant (Premium feature)
- Improve the App based on aggregated usage data
- Manage the community suggestions system

---

## 3. Data Storage and Security

### 3.1 Local storage

Your financial data, categories, and settings are stored locally on your device using SQLite. The App works 100% offline.

### 3.2 Cloud storage

If you create an account and have a Premium subscription, your data is synced with **Google Cloud Firestore** (a Firebase/Google Cloud service). Servers are located in the United States and follow Google Cloud Platform security standards.

### 3.3 Authentication

Account management is handled by **Firebase Authentication**. Your password is encrypted and we do not have access to it in plain text.

### 3.4 Payments

Subscriptions are processed directly by the **Apple App Store** or **Google Play Store** and managed by **RevenueCat**. We do not store payment data on our servers.

### 3.5 Financial data

The financial data you record in the App (accounts, transactions, cards) is information manually entered by you. The App **does not access** your real bank accounts, does not perform financial transactions, and does not connect to any financial institution.

---

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

- **Firebase (Google):** for authentication, cloud storage, and analytics.
- **RevenueCat:** for subscription management.
- **Apple / Google:** for payment processing in their respective stores.
- **AI provider chosen by you:** for processing AI assistant queries (see section 4.1 below).

All mentioned third parties operate under their own privacy policies and security standards.

### 4.1 AI Assistant — Data Sharing with the AI provider

The App offers an AI-powered financial assistant called "Mark." The assistant works on a **"bring your own key" (BYOK)** model: you choose a third-party AI provider and supply your own API key. Supported providers are **OpenAI (openai.com)**, **OpenRouter (openrouter.ai)**, **Anthropic (anthropic.com)**, and **Google Gemini (ai.google.dev)**. The in-app consent screen identifies, by name, the provider you selected before any data is sent.

When using this feature, the following data may be sent to the provider you configured:

- **Financial data:** account balances, transaction amounts, category names, transaction dates, recurrences, and financial projections — according to your privacy settings in the App.
- **Descriptions and notes:** transaction descriptions and personal notes — only if you enable these options in the privacy settings.
- **Conversation messages:** your questions and conversation history with the assistant.

**User controls:**
- Using the AI assistant requires your **explicit consent** before first use, with the selected provider identified by name.
- The assistant only works if you **configure a provider and your own API key**. The key is stored **only on your device** (the operating system's secure storage) and is never sent to our servers.
- You can choose **exactly which data categories** are shared through Mark's privacy settings.
- You can **revoke consent** at any time in settings, which immediately stops all data sharing.

**Third-party data retention:**
- Each provider processes data according to its **own privacy policy**. Refer to the terms of the provider you choose: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy), [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/privacy), [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy), [Google](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms). Because you use your own key, data is sent directly to the provider under the terms you accepted when obtaining the key.

## 5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** your data at any time through the App.
- **Correct** your data by editing your accounts, transactions, and categories.
- **Delete** your data by removing individual records or using the "Delete all data" function in settings.
- **Cancel** your subscription at any time through your store settings (App Store or Google Play).

To request complete deletion of your account and all associated data, visit the [account deletion page](delete-account) or contact us at: **capucholabs@gmail.com**

---

## 6. Data Retention

- **Local data:** remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.
- **Cloud data:** remains stored as long as your account exists. Upon requesting account deletion, all data will be removed within 30 days.
- **Usage metrics:** events are retained according to Firebase Analytics policies; the per-account usage summary is removed together with the account when you request deletion.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

MarkFinance is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not intentionally collect data from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete it.
